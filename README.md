# 🌍 Global Sales Dashboard – Power BI Project
![Global Sales]()
# 🔍 Overview
This project presents a dynamic Power BI dashboard offering a global perspective on sales, customer segments, market distribution, and product performance. It enables users to interact with sales data across regions, years, and segments, helping businesses understand their global footprint, identify top customers and products, and recognize loss-driving areas. The visualization is designed to support executive decision-making and sales optimization strategies.
# 🎯 Objectives
• To analyze total sales, order quantities, delivery performance, and returns globally.

• To examine sales by customer segments and geographic markets.

• To identify top 10 profitable customers and high-performing product categories.

• To detect loss-generating products for corrective actions.

• To visualize sales distribution by country and track performance across years.

• To assist in regional market planning and customer-focused targeting
# ❓ Business Questions Addressed
1. What is the total global sales and quantity ordered over the years?

2. Which segments (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) drive the most sales?

3. How are sales distributed across global markets (Asia Pacific, Europe, USCA, etc.)?

4. What is the average delivery time and number of returned orders?

5. Who are the top 10 most profitable customers?

6. Which products contribute most to profits?

7. Which products are causing losses, and by how much?

8. How does sales performance vary by year and location?
# 📌 Findings and Conclusion
• Total Global Sales: $12M with 169K units sold

• Average Delivery Time: 4 days

• Returned Orders: 1,079

• The Consumer segment leads with 51.5% of sales, followed by Corporate (30.2%) and Home Office (18.2%).

• The USCA market accounts for the largest share of global revenue (32.1%).

• Tamara Chand is the most profitable customer, generating over $9.1K in profit.

• Canon imageCLASS and Cisco Smart Switches are the most profitable products.

• Significant losses observed in Bevis Conference Table and Cubify Cube 3D Printer, suggesting need for inventory or pricing review.

• Sales are widely distributed across countries, with notable concentration in North America, Europe, and parts of Asia.
# Conclusion:
This dashboard helps organizations monitor international sales performance, identify high-value customers, and make informed decisions on product management. It is a crucial tool for aligning global operations with profitability goals.
# 📈 Strategic Insights Summary
The Global Sales Power BI dashboard offers actionable intelligence across regions, customer types, and product lines. By surfacing both high-performing and underperforming segments, it equips business leaders with the clarity needed to scale winning strategies and fix inefficiencies—ensuring sustainable growth in a competitive global market.
